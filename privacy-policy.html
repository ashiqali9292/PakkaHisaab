Privacy Policy

Effective date: 22 October 2026

In short

  • Pakka Hisaab works offline. Your books and entries are saved only on your phone.
  • We do not collect, upload, sell or share your personal or financial data.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking, and the app asks for no permissions.

1. Who we are

Pakka Hisaab ("the app") is developed and published on Google Play by Alien Wave Group ("we", "us"). This policy explains how the app handles your information.

2. Information stored on your device

To work as a cash book, the app saves what you type into it:

  • Book names
  • Entries: amount, cash in / cash out, date and time, remark, category and payment mode

This information is kept in the app's private storage on your phone. Other apps cannot read it, and it is never sent to us. We do not have servers that receive your data.

3. Information we collect

None. The app does not create an account and does not contain advertising or analytics software. If you choose to, you can pick a contact (to fill a party's name and number), attach a bill photo from your camera or gallery, or import a spreadsheet — these are saved only on your phone and never sent to us. The optional daily reminder only shows a notification on your phone.

4. Android backup

If Backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may copy the app's data to your own Google account so it can be restored on a new phone. This backup is handled by Google under Google's Privacy Policy; we cannot see or access it. You can turn backup off at any time in your phone's settings.

5. Sharing PDF reports

When you tap Share PDF report, the report is created on your phone and handed to the app you choose (for example WhatsApp, Gmail or Drive). We do not receive a copy. What happens next is governed by that app's own privacy policy.

6. Deleting your data

  • Delete any entry or book inside the app.
  • Uninstalling the app, or using Settings → Apps → Pakka Hisaab → Storage → Clear storage, permanently removes all of its data from your phone.
  • Android backups can be removed from your Google account settings.

Because we never hold your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

7. Security

Your data stays in storage that only the app can access. We recommend keeping a screen lock on your phone so that others cannot open your books.

8. Children

The app is meant for adults managing household or business money and is not directed at children under 13.

9. Changes to this policy

If the app changes how it handles data — for example if optional cloud sync is added in future — we will update this page and the effective date before that feature is released.
